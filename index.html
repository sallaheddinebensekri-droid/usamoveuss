Auto Insurance Coverage Types

Understanding what each coverage protects you from

⚖️

Liability Insurance

Liability auto insurance is the foundation of any policy and is required in almost every state. It covers damages and injuries you cause to others in an accident. Bodily injury liability pays for other people's medical bills, lost wages, and legal costs if you're sued. Property damage liability covers repairs to vehicles or property you damage. Without adequate liability auto insurance, you could be personally responsible for tens of thousands of dollars in damages.

🛡️

Comprehensive Coverage

Comprehensive auto insurance protects your vehicle from non-collision events — including theft, vandalism, fire, floods, hail, falling objects, and animal strikes. If your car is stolen or totaled by a natural disaster, comprehensive coverage pays to repair or replace it (minus your deductible). This coverage is typically required by lenders if you're financing or leasing your vehicle. MOVEUSS helps you find comprehensive auto insurance at the lowest available rate.

💥

Collision Coverage

Collision auto insurance pays to repair or replace your vehicle when it's damaged in an accident — whether you hit another car, a guardrail, a tree, or any other object. Unlike liability insurance, collision coverage protects your own vehicle regardless of who is at fault. It's especially important if your car has significant value. Our agents help you choose the right deductible to balance your monthly premium with your out-of-pocket costs.

🚨

Uninsured Motorist Coverage

Despite legal requirements, millions of American drivers are uninsured or underinsured. Uninsured motorist auto insurance protects you if you're hit by one of these drivers. It covers your medical expenses, lost wages, and sometimes vehicle damage when the at-fault driver has no insurance or insufficient coverage. In some states this coverage is mandatory. MOVEUSS strongly recommends uninsured motorist coverage as essential protection for all drivers.

🏥

Medical Payments Coverage

Medical payments (MedPay) auto insurance covers medical expenses for you and your passengers after an accident — regardless of who was at fault. It pays for hospital bills, surgeries, X-rays, ambulance fees, and even funeral expenses. MedPay is a relatively inexpensive add-on that provides valuable peace of mind, especially if you have a high-deductible health insurance plan. Ask our agents if MedPay makes sense for your situation.

📊

Gap Insurance

If your vehicle is totaled or stolen, standard auto insurance only pays the current market value — which may be far less than what you still owe on your loan. Gap insurance covers the difference between the insurance payout and your remaining loan balance, protecting you from being stuck making payments on a car you no longer have. Gap coverage is especially important for new vehicles, which depreciate rapidly. Our agents can add gap insurance to your policy affordably.

How Auto Insurance Works

Everything you need to know before getting covered

📋

What Is Auto Insurance?

Auto insurance is a contract between you and an insurance company in which you pay a monthly or semi-annual premium in exchange for financial protection against losses from accidents, theft, or other covered events. When you file an auto insurance claim, your insurer pays for covered damages — up to your policy limits — minus any deductible you owe. Auto insurance protects not just your vehicle, but your finances and legal liability as a driver. In most U.S. states, carrying minimum liability auto insurance is required by law.

💵

What Determines Your Rate?

Auto insurance companies use dozens of factors to calculate your premium. These include your driving record (accidents, violations, DUIs), your age and years of experience, your vehicle's make, model, year, and safety ratings, your annual mileage, where you live and park, your credit score (in most states), and your coverage selections and deductibles. Younger drivers and those with recent violations typically pay higher rates. MOVEUSS compares 50+ carriers to find which insurer rates your specific profile most favorably.

🔍

How Much Auto Insurance Do You Need?

The right amount of auto insurance depends on your specific financial situation, vehicle value, and risk tolerance. Every driver needs at minimum the liability limits required by their state — but these minimums are often dangerously low. If you're involved in a serious accident, state minimum auto insurance limits may not cover all damages, leaving you personally responsible for the difference. Drivers with assets to protect should carry higher liability limits of at least $100,000 per person and $300,000 per accident. If your vehicle is financed or leased, your lender likely requires comprehensive and collision coverage. Our licensed MOVEUSS agents help you assess exactly how much auto insurance you need so you're fully protected without overpaying.
