Health Insurance Plan Types

Understanding the main kinds of plans and how they differ

🏥

HMO Plans

Health Maintenance Organization plans usually require you to choose a primary care doctor and get referrals to see specialists. You generally must use in-network providers, except in emergencies. HMOs often have lower monthly premiums and predictable copays, which makes them a popular choice for people who want to keep costs down and do not mind staying within a network.

🩺

PPO Plans

Preferred Provider Organization plans give you more freedom to see doctors and specialists without referrals, both in and out of network. Out-of-network care usually costs more, and premiums tend to be higher than HMOs. PPOs suit people who want flexibility, travel often, or want to keep a specific doctor who may not be in a smaller network.

🔗

EPO Plans

Exclusive Provider Organization plans combine features of both: you typically do not need referrals, but you must stay inside the plan's network for non-emergency care. Premiums are often moderate. Our agents help you check whether your doctors, hospitals and prescriptions are included before you choose.

💳

High-Deductible Plans & HSAs

High-deductible health plans usually have lower monthly premiums but higher deductibles. Some qualify for a Health Savings Account (HSA), which lets you set aside pre-tax money for medical expenses. They can work well for healthy people who want lower premiums and a tax-advantaged way to save, but you should be comfortable paying more out of pocket before coverage kicks in.

🧓

Medicare Plans

Medicare is federal health coverage generally for people 65 and older and some younger people with qualifying conditions. Beyond Original Medicare, many people compare Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement (Medigap) and Part D prescription plans. Our licensed agents can explain the differences so you can choose what fits your needs.

📅

Short-Term & Other Options

If you are between jobs or outside an enrollment period, other options may exist. Short-term plans generally offer limited benefits and may not cover pre-existing conditions or meet ACA standards, so it is important to understand exactly what they do and do not cover. Your agent will explain the tradeoffs clearly.

How Health Insurance Works

Everything you need to know before getting covered

📋

What Is Health Insurance?

Health insurance is a contract between you and an insurance company in which you pay a monthly premium in exchange for help paying medical costs. Depending on your plan, coverage can include doctor visits, hospital stays, emergency care, prescriptions, preventive care and more. When you use care, you typically share the cost through deductibles, copays and coinsurance, up to your plan's out-of-pocket maximum.

💵

What Determines Your Cost?

Your cost depends on the plan you choose and where you live. Key factors include the plan's metal tier (Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum), your age, your zip code, your household size, and tobacco use. Your income may also affect whether you qualify for premium tax credits or cost-sharing reductions. MOVEUSS compares 50+ carriers to help you see which plans may fit your profile.

🔍

How Much Coverage Do You Need?

The right plan depends on how often you use care, which doctors and prescriptions you rely on, and how much financial risk you are comfortable with. A lower premium often means a higher deductible, and a higher premium often means lower costs when you need care. Check the out-of-pocket maximum, since that is the most you would pay for covered in-network care in a year. Our licensed MOVEUSS agents help you weigh these tradeoffs so you are protected without overpaying.
